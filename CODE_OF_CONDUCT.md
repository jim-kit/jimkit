# Code of conduct

We want Jimkit to be a place where people can ask questions, report problems and send changes without being treated badly.

- Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or sharing other people's private information.
- Keep discussion on topic and constructive. Critique code and designs freely; keep it respectful.
- Maintainers may remove comments, close threads and block people who break these rules.

If someone breaks these rules, tell the maintainers privately at jimkit@thejimmy.dev. Reports are read by the maintainers only,
and the person who reported is not named to the person reported.
